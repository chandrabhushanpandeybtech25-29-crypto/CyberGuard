    🔐 Essential Safety Tips

    🔑

    Strong Passwords

    Use long, unique passwords for every account. Consider a password manager and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).

    🎣

    Identify Phishing

    Be cautious of urgent messages, unexpected attachments, fake login pages, and requests for passwords or OTPs.

    🔗

    Check Suspicious Links

    Examine the actual domain before clicking. Watch for misspellings, misleading subdomains, and unexpected redirects.

    📱

    QR Code Safety

    Preview the destination before opening it. Never enter banking details or passwords on an untrusted website opened through a QR code.

    🔒

    Protect Your Privacy

    Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or recovery codes. Share personal and financial information only when necessary and with trusted services.

    🔄

    Keep Devices Updated

    Install security updates, use genuine software, review app permissions, and remove applications you no longer need.

    🚨 What Should I Do If Something Goes Wrong?

    Clicked a suspicious link?

    Stop interacting with the website. Do not download files or enter credentials. If you entered a password, change it from a trusted device and secure the account.

    Shared an OTP or banking details?

    Contact your bank or service provider immediately through its official channel. Secure the affected account and report any unauthorised transactions.

    Lost money in an online fraud in India?

    Contact your bank immediately and call 1930 to report financial cyber fraud. You can also submit a complaint through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
